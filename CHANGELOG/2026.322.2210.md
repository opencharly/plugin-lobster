### Why this is a pin bump and not a regeneration

The committed `params/cue_types_gen.go` carries **0 `yaml:` tags**. I first read that as the pre-yaml generator's fingerprint and regenerated it — which turned this PR's own `ci.yml` **red**:

```
go  Generated params are reproducible (cue:gen)
<   InputTokens any `json:"inputTokens"`          ← what cue exp gengotypes produces
>   InputTokens any `yaml:"inputTokens,omitempty"` ← what I had committed
```

**The cause is this repo's own `ci.yml`**: it has a `cue:gen` step that regenerates with `cue exp gengotypes` (JSON-only, no tag normalization) and diffs the committed artifact. So the JSON-only form here is **enforced**, not stale — `0 yaml:` in this repo means "the canonical form is required", not "an older generator built it".

**Measured across the wave**: `plugin-lobster` is the only one of `adb`, `cua`, `nerdctl`, `omarchy`, `lobster` whose `ci.yml` carries a `cue:gen` step. That is why the same fingerprint means different things in different repos.

So the regeneration and the new `schemaparams` gate are **reverted**; the artifact is byte-identical (`git diff --stat origin/main...HEAD` shows only `go.mod` and `go.sum`), and the repo keeps the gate it already had.

**This also qualifies the wave's discriminator** (opencharly/opencharly#1109): a zero `yaml:` count means **either** a pre-yaml vintage **or** a repo whose CI enforces the JSON-only form. The two are indistinguishable from the artifact alone; the `ci.yml` decides.
